[b]Better Fines[/b]

Looking for more driving realism?

[b]Better Fines[/b] adds realistic traffic enforcement to Big Ambitions: speeding tickets, red-light cameras, wrong-way detection, pedestrian collision tickets (with [b]Better Pedestrians[/b]), government SMS notices, repeat-offense surcharges, and driver's license suspension.

Subscribe on Steam Workshop, then enable [b]Better Fines[/b] and [b]LIB BA Player Location[/b] in the in-game [b]Mods[/b] menu.

[b]Localization:[/b] All [b]22[/b] Big Ambitions interface languages supported.

[b]Source code and updates:[/b] [url=https://github.com/capisoft-lib/BigAmbitions_BetterFines]GitHub — capisoft-lib/BigAmbitions_BetterFines[/url]

[b]Features[/b]

[list]
[*][b]Speeding fines[/b] — city limit [b]50 km/h[/b], highway/bridge [b]80 km/h[/b]; enforced after [b]+10%[/b] over the limit with an on-screen warning
[*][b]Red-light cameras[/b] — optional visual flash on ticket; orange-light fines off by default
[*][b]Wrong-way detection[/b] — fines for driving against traffic
[*][b]Pedestrian hit fines[/b] — with [b]Better Pedestrians[/b] (recommended companion mod)
[*][b]Government SMS[/b] — ticket and notice messages in-game
[*][b]Repeat-offense surcharge[/b] — +50% at 3 active fines, +100% at 5 (rolling window)
[*][b]License suspension[/b] — after 10 active fines until tickets expire
[*][b]Fines status panel[/b] — active tickets summary in the HUD
[/list]


[b]Options (ESC → Options → Mods)[/b]

[list]
[*]Fine amount: fixed dollar amount or % of yesterday's supplier margin
[*]Toggle each fine type, visual flash, and license suspension
[*]Defaults: all [b]on[/b] except orange-light fines ([b]off[/b])
[/list]


[b]Requirements[/b]

[list]
[*][b]LIB BA Player Location[/b] — required (Workshop)
[*][b]LIB BA Unified UI[/b] — bundled in [b]Better Fines/Dependencies/[/b] (no separate mod)
[*][b]Better Pedestrians[/b] — optional; needed for pedestrian hit fines
[*][b]Speedometer[/b] — optional HUD to see your speed
[/list]
